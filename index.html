Services

What we fix, and how.

Three systems. Each one solves a problem logistics owners already feel.

01

Google Business Profile Recovery & Reinstatement

For suspended, disabled, flagged or unverified listings. We find out why it happened, then get it back and keep it safe.

Ideal for: owners whose listing vanished or was suspended.

  • Diagnosing the suspension reason
  • Evidence: documents, signage, proof of location
  • Submitting and following up on the appeal
  • Fixing the cause so it doesn't repeat
  • Verification help
02

Profile Setup, Optimization & Map Ranking (Local SEO)

We build your listing the way Google likes it, then push it up the map. You show up for the searches that bring jobs.

Ideal for: companies on the map but outside the top 3, and startups launching their listing.

  • Correct primary and secondary categories
  • Services, areas and hours
  • Truck and warehouse photos, Posts, Q&A
  • Citation and NAP cleanup
  • Keywords for "near me" searches
  • Competitor ranking analysis
  • Monthly performance report
03

Review Management: Removal Requests & Review Growth

Bad reviews get handled the right way. Good customers get asked, automatically, after every job.

Ideal for: companies hurt by unfair reviews or outrun by competitors' ratings.

  • Auditing your reviews
  • Removal requests for policy-breaking reviews
  • Professional reply templates
  • Automatic review requests after every job

Google decides what gets removed. We submit strong, policy-based requests and never promise a specific outcome.
